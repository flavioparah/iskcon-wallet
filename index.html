Devotees, pilgrims, and farmers face friction converting currencies and managing cross-border revenue. The missing piece is financial integration.

Visits holy pilgrimage sites every year but struggles to convert currency and make local payments easily.

Russian Monk

Needs to export rural produce to interior networks with transparent foreign revenue handling.

Stellar-Based Solution

ISKCON Wallet & Bhakti Currency

Focused on financial inclusion, the Stellar-based solution enables remote access to the spiritual economy: education, commerce, tourism, and rural projects.

  • Fiat/Stellar/Fiat instant conversion (Pix, UPI, SEPA, FedNow)
  • Bhakti Currency cashback & app wallet integration

Bhakti Currency (BKT)

"Bhakti", from Sanskrit, means "Love." A cryptocurrency minted on Stellar, with a deflationary value designed to foster membership, reward volunteers (Proof of Bhakti), and fund spiritual projects transparently.

04 — Market Opportunity

Digitizing an Existing Economy

The market opportunity lies in unifying and optimizing the flow of value within a global community that has existed and been economically active for 59 years, but is fragmented by traditional financial systems.

We are not creating a market from scratch, but rather digitizing and integrating an already functioning economy, capturing the currency that already flows between members, centers, and projects globally.

Ecosystem Integration
